Word frequency data — Wortschatz Leipzig Corpora Collection
===========================================================

The frequency value stored beside each word in this application's dictionaries is derived from
the aggregate word-count tables of the Leipzig Corpora Collection:

    eng_news_2020_100K
    rus_news_2022_100K

Attribution
-----------

Word frequency data derived from the Wortschatz Leipzig Corpora Collection
(https://wortschatz.uni-leipzig.de/), Natural Language Processing Group,
Leipzig University.

Reference:

    D. Goldhahn, T. Eckart, U. Quasthoff.
    Building Large Monolingual Dictionaries at the Leipzig Corpora Collection:
    From 100 to 200 Languages.
    Proceedings of the 8th International Conference on Language Resources and
    Evaluation (LREC'12), 2012.

Statement of changes
--------------------

Only the aggregate word/count column of the corpus was read. Counts were folded to lowercase and
summed, and were retained solely for words that already appear in this application's own word
lists (which come from separate sources — see the SCOWL and Lebedev notices). No corpus
sentences, documents or source URLs are included in this application.

The exact procedure is recorded in tools/build_frequencies.py in the project's source
repository.
